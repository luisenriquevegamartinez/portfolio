  decision: >-

    PoC with Angular and Module Federation, aimed at multi-tenancy and at reusing the existing

    build-time asset and per-tenant theming mechanism with AngularJS and Gulp.
  result: >-

    let text, style palettes and resources differ per client without polluting the shared
    codebase with conditional logic.
